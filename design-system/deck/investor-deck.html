monolite 02 · THESIS
02 / 14SERIES A · APR 2026
Thesis
The next decade of B2B software is not built around the user.
It is built around the company.

For thirty years, SaaS optimised the seat. One person, one login, one dashboard. The company was an aggregation of seats.

AI inverts this. The company is the unit. Agents work on its data, in its context, with its memory. Seats become observers.
