§ 02 · Brand

Voice, spoken quietly.

Style guide · Apr 2026

We write the way a senior accountant speaks to a client they respect — clearly, briefly, without flourish.

i.

Quiet, not silent.

We say only what is needed. But we say it. Empty UI is not the same as silent UI. A blank inbox earns one sentence.

ii.

Specific, not generic.

"Seven agents ran" not "everything is fine". Names, numbers, timestamps. The product reads like a ledger because it is one.

iii.

Italian, then English.

We write Italian first because that is where the work is. English is the translation, never the source. We do not call it "AI". We call it "agente".
